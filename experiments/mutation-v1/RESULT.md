sought in the first fixture yielding a REFUTED subject, and also that a role
is `NO_SUBJECT` only if absent across all fixtures. The contradiction was
noticed after the scans, so neither reading is chosen: choosing the one under
which `mod` furnishes a full triangle would be post-result tuning.

What v1 established, and why it is kept unchanged at `71f313f`:

  method-only mutation style, not architectural ownership. groupcache's
  `HTTPPoolOptions.BasePath` is the counterexample: a constructor in the
  declaring package initialising its own options object is not an
  unauthorised mutation because it is not a method. 30 of `mod`'s 34
  refutations are of that kind; 4 are cross-package.
- The sealed tool works and its verdicts are reproducible (sha256
  `d136e99b…`, both scans recorded, every passed-over verdict kept).

independent role-selection rule. Nothing observed here pre-selects a subject
